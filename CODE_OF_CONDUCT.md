# Code of Conduct

Softromeda Screen Recorder is built by people from many countries, who speak many languages
and have different levels of experience. We want taking part in the project to be a friendly,
welcoming experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

* Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
* Respect differing opinions and experiences; disagree about ideas, not about people.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected and learn from them.
* Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Insults, personal attacks, and derogatory or discriminatory comments.
* Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwelcome sexual attention.
* Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
* Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or email, without their
  permission.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the GitHub repository (issues, pull
requests, discussions and reviews), and anywhere someone represents the project, such as in
official channels or at events.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the maintainers at
**contact@softromeda.com**. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential; the
identity of the person reporting is not shared without their permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide what action to take for any behavior they consider inappropriate. In
order of severity, this can be:

1. **A private warning**, explaining what was inappropriate and why.
2. **A public warning**, with a request to stop, and possibly to apologise.
3. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
4. **A permanent ban** from the project, for serious or repeated violations.

Maintainers may also edit, hide or delete comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code of conduct.
